01 / ABOUT

A little
about me.

Hello, beautiful people! My name is Airene, and I’m a teen who loves making art and sharing it with the world. Lately, I’ve come to the conclusion that our world has gone a little black and white. And clearly, it needs more colour. Now, yes, there are already plenty of artists out there. But what makes me special? Well... absolutely nothing, really. I’m simply an artist who loves creating things and, hopefully, adding a little more colour to your day. I want to make art that brings back that feeling of seeing the world through a child’s eyes, In my very important opinion, which everyone obviously cares about, art is kind of like cheese on pasta or chocolate chips in cookies. Once you have it in your life, you’ll wonder how you ever lived without it. So go have a look around, explore my collection, and maybe find something that adds a little colour to your world. In conclusion, I love art, I love yapping, and I’m probably exceptionally good at wasting people's time. So, if you’d like to chat, ask me something, commission some art, or just say hi, send me an email or a message. — Airene
